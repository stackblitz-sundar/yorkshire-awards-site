Our purpose

Celebrating the heart of Yorkshire.

Every community has people who quietly create extraordinary change. These awards are an opportunity to recognise their commitment and share the stories that inspire others.

From grassroots volunteers and young leaders to educators, artists, cultural ambassadors, environmental advocates and socially responsible businesses, we celebrate contributions that make Yorkshire a more connected and welcoming place.

The awards welcome nominations representing the breadth of communities and cultures across Yorkshire.

Transparency & information

Information and policies.

These are introductory website notices. Formal nomination rules, privacy information and event terms will be published before any personal information is collected or tickets are offered.

Yorkshire Community & Cultural Excellence Awards is an independently planned community recognition initiative. It is not an official Yorkshire local authority awards programme and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sheffield City Council or the Lord Mayor Awards. Event details remain subject to confirmation.

Nominations are not currently being accepted through this website. Full eligibility conditions, submission instructions, judging criteria, conflict-of-interest safeguards, deadlines and any applicable restrictions will be made available before nominations open. No award or outcome is guaranteed.

This launch website does not provide a nomination form or request personal information directly. A full privacy notice identifying the legal organiser, contact details, processing purposes, lawful bases, recipients, retention periods and individual rights must be published before nomination or contact forms are activated. Third-party services such as web hosting and externally loaded fonts may process technical information according to their own policies.

We aim to provide a clear, accessible experience across mobile and desktop devices. Information on this website may be updated as plans develop. If you encounter an accessibility issue, please contact the organiser using the published contact details once available.
